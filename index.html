About Me

Where Customers Meet Technology

I work at the intersection of customers, technology and operations.

I have a background in Education and hands-on experience across Customer Success, client onboarding, customer support, technical support and operations. I also have practical Cloud & DevOps experience across AWS, Kubernetes, Terraform, Docker, CI/CD and GitOps.

My approach is grounded in understanding what the customer is trying to achieve, identifying what is getting in the way, and coordinating the right next step. That can mean resolving a platform issue, supporting onboarding, documenting a problem clearly, following up with a customer, or working across technical and operational teams.

I am focused on customer-facing technology roles where strong communication and technical understanding need to work together — particularly Customer Success and Technical Account Management.
