Case Study · Customer Experience & Onboarding

Sunrise Digital MFB — Customer Experience & Onboarding

A customer-journey review focused on reducing onboarding friction, improving activation and creating a clearer path to first value.

RoleCustomer Success & Customer Experience
FocusOnboarding · Activation · VoC
ToolsHubSpot · Airtable · Miro · Zapier · Figma

The challenge

42,600 customers registered, but only 5,120 were active after 30 days. The review traced the drop-off through verification, account setup and first funding, then connected the journey data with customer feedback to identify the moments that needed the most attention.

What I found

Activation was the real gap

Only 12% of registrants were active after 30 days, with major losses around BVN/NIN verification and the final activation steps.

Customers lacked visibility

There was no clear save-and-resume path, confirmation/status communication or proactive contact when a customer stalled.

Feedback needed an owner

461+ feedback items came through care calls, app reviews, social comments and branch notes without one consolidated loop.

The customer journey I redesigned

Register → Verify → Save & Resume → Understand Account Tier → Account Ready → Fund → First Transaction → 30-Day Engagement

The redesign introduced save/resume, instant document/status confirmation, clearer tier language, account-ready confirmation and proactive contact when a customer had not funded within 24 hours.

How I would measure it

First-deposit conversion

47% → 65% proposed pilot target.

Funded → 2+ transactions

55% → 65% proposed pilot target.

End-to-end activation

12% → 25% proposed target, to be validated through a pilot.

VoC loop

Collect → Centralise → Categorise → Prioritise → Assign owner/SLA → Act → Close the loop → Measure.

The pilot connected a feedback form to Airtable and email alerts, with HubSpot as the target state for routing issues to Payments, Engineering, Support and Customer Experience.
