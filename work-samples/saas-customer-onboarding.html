The starting point

For this portfolio exercise, I imagined a new customer signing up for a workflow SaaS product. The contract is signed, but the real work is making sure the customer actually gets value from what they bought.

My focus was the first 30 days: understand the customer's goal, get the product ready, train the right people, watch adoption and finish with a clear next step.

Portfolio simulation. The customer, figures and targets are illustrative and are included to show how I would structure an onboarding journey.

How I worked through it

1

Start with the reason they bought

I would review the handoff, confirm the customer's goals and agree what “success” should look like before getting into setup.

2

Get the basics right

Workspace settings, roles, integrations and data come next, with a simple readiness checklist shared with the customer.

3

Make training usable

Rather than one long session, I would use role-based training, short guides and a knowledge base the team can return to later.

4

Watch what people actually use

Login and feature-use signals help show where adoption is moving and where a customer may need another conversation.

5

Close the loop

The first value review brings the results back to the original goals and gives both sides a clear next step.
