Technical Account Review

A quarterly review connecting customer goals, product usage, technical blockers and a clear action plan.

RoleTechnical Account Management case study
TimelineQuarterly review
ToolsGainsight-style CS platform · Jira · Salesforce · Zendesk

The starting point

I built this around a customer who is getting value from the product, but not evenly across the business. A few technical issues are also starting to get in the way.

The point is not to hand the customer a list of problems. It is to connect those problems to the outcome they care about, agree what matters most and leave everyone with clear owners.

Illustrative account view created to demonstrate how I structure a technical account review.

How I worked through it

1

Start with the customer goal

I would pull the customer's business goals, usage information, open tickets and technical work into the same view.

2

Look at adoption

I would compare how different teams use the product against what they expected to achieve with it.

3

Separate noise from blockers

Issues are grouped by impact, root cause and owner so urgent problems do not get buried in a long list.

4

Agree the next three moves

Instead of giving the customer a huge action list, I would prioritise a few actions with clear owners and dates.

5

Follow through

The account plan stays useful only if the actions are revisited, updated and connected to the next customer conversation.
