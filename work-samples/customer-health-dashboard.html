Customer Health & Retention Dashboard

A practical health model that flags healthy, watch and at-risk accounts early enough to act.

RoleCustomer Success case study
TimelineMonthly cycle
ToolsGainsight-style CS platform · Excel / Sheets · HubSpot · Intercom

The starting point

I wanted to show how I would notice a customer drifting into risk early, rather than waiting for the renewal conversation to expose it.

I combined four signals — product usage, engagement, support activity and sentiment — and turned them into a view a CS team could scan quickly and act on.

Portfolio simulation. All accounts, names and figures are illustrative sample data created to demonstrate the approach.

How I worked through it

1

Choose signals that tell a story

I used product usage, engagement, support health and sentiment because a single metric rarely explains what is happening with a customer.

2

Make risk easy to see

Healthy, Watch and At Risk bands turn a long account list into something the team can scan quickly.

3

Show the reason behind the score

A score is more useful when the team can see what changed and why an account needs attention.

4

Connect the signal to an action

Each risk band has a practical response, from re-engagement and training to an executive conversation.

5

Keep reviewing it

I would compare the health trend with customer outcomes over time and adjust the model when the evidence says it needs changing.
