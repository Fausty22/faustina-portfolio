Case Study · Customer Success Strategy

Okonkwo FinTech — Customer Health & Retention

A scalable Customer Success process that turns product-usage changes into risk signals, prioritised attention and practical customer action.

RoleCustomer Success Strategy & Operations
FocusHealth scoring · Risk · Retention
ToolsHubSpot · Gmail · Excel · Claude AI · Zendesk

The challenge

The case starts with a customer whose reconciliation usage stopped for five weeks. The important Customer Success question is not simply “what changed?” but “why did it change, what does it mean for the customer, and what should we do next?”

The framework

Monitor → Detect → Assess → Segment → Prioritise → Route → Intervene → Measure

Usage data provides an early signal. The CSM then adds customer context, investigates the reason, decides the right intervention and measures the outcome.

Health score model

Breadth · 35%

How widely the product is being used across the account.

Recency · 30%

How recently meaningful product activity occurred.

Volume · 20%

How much product activity is taking place.

Sentiment · 15%

What the customer experience is telling the team.

Health bands: 80–100 Healthy · 50–79 Monitor · Below 50 At Risk.

Prioritising attention

Healthy accounts can receive automated education. Accounts needing attention receive a nudge and monitoring. At-risk accounts move to CSM outreach and recovery. High-value, at-risk customers receive same-day attention.

Priority is based on risk severity × customer value × renewal proximity × number of signals.

The working automation

A live HubSpot workflow was built to move from a health-score threshold to a CSM alert and check-in task. The demonstration uses simulated customer/product data, while the workflow and account/segment mechanics are shown as working artefacts.

The demo produced an at-risk filter with 353 of 1,000 simulated accounts flagged and re-scored as usage data changes.

Customer Success vs Support

Support closes loops. Customer Success opens conversations. Support handles bounded technical or access issues; Customer Success looks at adoption, health, retention and the broader customer outcome, bringing Support in when a technical issue is part of the risk.
